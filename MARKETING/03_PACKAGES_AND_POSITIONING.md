## 1. Positioning

**Headline:** Pure-veg farm stay and celebration venue near Sriperumbudur & Kanchipuram.

**Sub:** 60–90 minutes from Chennai. Rooms, lawns, rooftop, pool, rain dance, sports, Gaushala.

**For whom:** Families, traditional functions, factory-belt corporates, Kanchipuram pilgrims.

**Not for:** Beach weekenders, non-veg groups, nightlife, last-minute walk-in parties that ignore veg rules.

**Proof points (use in every listing):**

- Pure vegetarian kitchen (say it first)
- Gaushala on site
- Pool + jacuzzi + kids splash
- Rain dance, bonfire, box cricket, volleyball, indoor games
- Lawn + rooftop / garden terrace for functions
- Large rooms (reviews already say this — keep showing two queen beds if that is true)

**Geo line (fix the website):**  
Kilachery / Kannur Village, 602108 — near Sriperumbudur. Not OMR Perumbakkam.

**Drive-time line (measure in Week 1 and lock):**  
~40 min Sriperumbudur SIPCOT · ~40–50 min Kanchipuram · ~60–90 min west / south-west Chennai · confirm Airport.

## 3. Package skeletons

### Sunday Family Farm (lead consumer product)

- 1 room, 2N optional upgrade
- Breakfast + 1 main meal
- Pool + kids area
- 45-minute Gaushala
- Indoor games
- **Add:** bonfire, rain dance slot, extra beds  
**Sell on:** WhatsApp, Airbnb, Instagram.

### Sriperumbudur Offsite (lead weekday product)

- Day or 1N
- Veg buffet + tea
- 1 huddle space
- Cricket **or** Gaushala CSR hour
- 12% off on company invoice  
**Sell on:** HR calls, PDF, LinkedIn later.  
**Do not** put “party” in the subject line. Use “offsite / visitor stay / L&D day”.

### Kanchi Halt

- Room + satvik dinner + early breakfast
- Quiet floor if possible
- Temple-distance note  
**Sell on:** yatra operators, Kanchipuram planners.

### Birthday / Engagement 4-hour

- Lawn or rooftop
- Veg menu slab (50 / 80 / 120)
- Sound + basic lights
- Optional rain dance
- Hard stop + cleanup buffer so in-house guests still sleep  
**Sell on:** photographers, décor partners, Instagram.

### Wedding block

- Date hold on 40%  
- Room block mandatory for exclusive
- 2 veg menu tiers
- Bonfire optional night-before
- Rain plan (indoor games + indoor dining — write the real rain plan)  
**Sell on:** planners + FAM only. Do not discount exclusivity.

### Pre-wedding sunrise slot

- 6:00–10:00
- Lawn + Gaushala + one room for change
- ₹8–15k or complimentary for active partners  
**Purpose:** photographer channel, not revenue.

### Rain Dance Sunday (day)

- Entry + lunch + timed slot
- Good for groups that will not take rooms  
**Watch:** noise, neighbours, music licence.

## 6. Veg-only: how to say it so you lose the wrong guest early

> “Our kitchen is fully vegetarian — no egg, no meat, no fish. Traditional functions, family stays, and corporate offsites that want a satvik kitchen choose us for that reason.”

Put this on the website hero, WhatsApp catalog, OTA house rules, and the first quote email. A lost enquiry here is cheaper than an angry function.
