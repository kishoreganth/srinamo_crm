## 2. How money is made here

Four engines. Run all four. Weekends alone will never fill 40 rooms.

| Engine | Who / when | Ticket (working) | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Room nights | Families, pilgrims · Fri–Sun + holidays | ₹4,500–7,500 / room | Base occupancy |
| B. Day / buyout | Friends, birthdays · Sat–Sun | ₹45k–1.5L day | High yield (lawn/pool/rain dance) |
| C. Events | Wedding, engagement, reception · season | ₹2L–12L+ | Highest profit + room block |
| D. Corporate weekday | HR / Admin / L&D · Mon–Thu | ₹2,000–3,500 / pax | Occupancy fixer |

**Gaushala, farm walk, bonfire, cricket, cooking class** are not separate businesses in Year 1. They are add-ons that raise ADR and make the listing photographable.

**Alcohol / bar counter:** if a bar exists, write a clear policy (permit, timings, outside liquor, corkage). Corporates and traditional weddings will ask in the first call.

### Phase 0 — Foundation (Week 1–2)

**Goal:** One source of truth, one rate card, one story, one photo set.

**You do:**

1. **Export the notebook and Excel** into the PMS in this repo (guests, bookings, advances, pending balances). Stop dual-writing after go-live.
2. **Write a one-page rate card** (published + trade). Rooms weekday/weekend, extra adult/child, day picnic, pool-only, rain dance, bonfire, Gaushala add-on, lawn hire, rooftop hire, wedding / engagement / birthday packages, corporate per-pax, exclusive buyout. GST inclusive or exclusive — pick one and stay consistent.
3. **Payment policy on one page.** Suggested default: 30% to confirm, 70% 48 hours before arrival for rooms; 40% to block date + 40% at T-15 days + 20% before function for events. Soften “full amount at check-in” — reviews already punish this.
4. **Professional shoot (1 full day + 1 night).** Rooms, pool/jacuzzi/kids, rain dance in use, lawn empty + dressed, rooftop, cricket, volleyball, indoor games, Gaushala with cows and kids, veg thali, bonfire, aerial if drone is allowed. You cannot list Airbnb or sell weddings on phone photos.
5. **Claim and clean Google Business Profile.** Correct name, Kilachery / Kannur pin (not OMR Perumbakkam), categories: Farmstay + Banquet hall + Resort. Hours, WhatsApp, website, 20 photos, weekly posts. This is the cheapest demand channel in India.
6. **Fix the website in one sprint.** Delete theme junk pages. Tamil + English only. Visible prices or “from ₹X”. WhatsApp click-to-chat. Three CTAs: Stay / Celebrate / Corporate. Map + drive time from Chennai, Sriperumbudur, Kanchipuram, Tambaram, Ambattur.
7. **Licenses checklist:** GST, FSSAI, fire NOC for gatherings, music (PPL/IPRS) if you play recorded music at events, pool safety, generator, parking count. Marketing events without this is how a good month gets shut down.
8. **Service SOP for the two named caretakers and whoever is “manager”.** Greeting, 15-minute check-in, manager visible at arrival, no argument over remaining payment if advance is clean.

**Exit gate:** Rate card signed. 80+ usable photos. GBP claimed. Excel imported or import date locked. Website junk removed. WhatsApp Business app live with greeting + catalog.

### Phase 3 — B2B outbound (Week 6–16) — this is the revenue engine

**Goal:** Other people’s clients fill your lawn and your Tuesday.

You said you will call anyone in the Chennai region. Good. Treat it as a sales job with a list, a script, and a weekly FAM visit. Do not “network” without a tracker.

**Call volume that works:** 40 quality conversations a week, not 400 random numbers. 8 conversations a day × 5 days.

#### Who to call (in this order)

1. **Wedding / event planners** — Chennai, Kanchipuram, Tambaram, Porur, Ambattur, Avadi. They already have dates and budgets.
2. **Wedding photographers and cinematographers** — they influence venue after the couple shortlists. Fastest “yes” if you give them a content day.
3. **Birthday / kids / family event planners** and **decorators** (balloon, florists, sound).
4. **Corporate HR, Admin, L&D, Admin assistants** in Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Irungattukottai, Sri City-adjacent vendors, Ambattur industrial estate, Guindy/OMR companies that do offsites west of the city.
5. **Travel desks / TPAs** that book room blocks for factory visitors.
6. **Temple / yatra operators** running Kanchipuram + Tirupati circuits who need a clean veg night halt.
7. **School / college admin** (weekday picnic, sports day) after corporates are moving.
8. **Freelancers later (Phase 5)** — appointment setters on 8% of closed weekday corporate, or a monthly retainer + 3%.

Named corporate belt (start here, then expand): Hyundai, Foxconn / Bharat FIH, Samsung, Saint-Gobain, Nokia, Dell, Flex, Jabil, Apollo Tyres, Danfoss, Visteon, and Oragadam names (Renault Nissan, Daimler ecosystem, suppliers). You do not need the CHRO. You need **Admin / HR executive / L&D coordinator**.

#### Offer they can say yes to in one call

Pick **one** per partner type. Do not negotiate a new deal every time.

| Partner | Default offer | When you pay |
|---|---|---|
| Event planner | 10% of venue + room block (not F&B, or 5% on F&B if they bring catering control) | 7 days after guest balance is cleared |
| Photographer | ₹3,000 per confirmed stay/event they referred, or 8% of room+venue | Same |
| Decorator / DJ / makeup | 8% or reciprocal preferred-vendor list | Same |
| Corporate HR | **No cash to employee.** Guest org gets 12% weekday off + free Gaushala / cricket block. Optional CSR tree / goseva add-on. | Discount on invoice |
| Travel agent | 12% on rooms | Monthly statement |
| Guest (no partner) | 10% weekday / 5% weekend if they book direct after a review | On the folio |

**FAM trip (do this every second Saturday, 10:30–14:00):** 8–12 planners/photographers. Pool + thali + lawn walk + rooftop + Gaushala. They leave with a one-pager, rate card, and your WhatsApp. Budget ₹1,500–2,000/head. This closes more venues than 200 cold calls.

**Tracker columns:** name, firm, phone, type, date called, result, next step, FAM invited, first booking date, commission due.

**Exit gate:** 80 partners in the sheet. 25 have visited. 8 have sent a first enquiry. 2 corporates have a weekday hold. Commission policy printed and used without exception.
