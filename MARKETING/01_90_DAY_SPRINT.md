# 90-Day Sprint — Start Marketing

**Print:** A4 · File → Print → Save as PDF, or open `pdf/01_90_DAY_SPRINT.pdf`  
**Daily ticks + takeover + grey questions:** `04_START_CHECKLIST.md`  
**Enquiry channels + partner ops:** `05_ENQUIRY_AND_PARTNER_OPS.md`

Use this as the weekly runbook. Mark each line done. Do not skip Week 1–2 to “go do Instagram”.

**North-star for Day 90**

- Weekday occupancy ≥ 22% (from a typical ~10–15% empty-weekday pattern)
- Weekend occupancy ≥ 65%
- 2 named packages selling on WhatsApp
- Airbnb + 1 Indian OTA live
- 80 partners in the sheet, 25 have visited
- Review-ask running on every checkout
- Sunday scoreboard going to owners without fail

---

## Week 1 — Truth and the desk

- [ ] Collect notebook + Excel. List every future booking and every advance.
- [ ] Open a Sales sheet: Name, Phone, Source, Occasion, Dates, Pax, Value, Stage, Next action, Owner.
- [ ] Draft rate card v0 (rooms weekday/weekend, day picnic, event, corporate, buyout).
- [ ] Draft payment policy (kill “full cash at check-in” as the only option).
- [ ] Claim Google Business Profile. Fix address to Kilachery / Kannur, 602108.
- [ ] WhatsApp Business: greeting, away message, 4 quick replies (Stay / Birthday / Wedding / Corporate).
- [ ] Walk the property with a phone: 40 photos, 8 videos (pool, rain dance, Gaushala, cricket, food, room, lawn, rooftop).
- [ ] Write the 2-sentence positioning and put it on WhatsApp + GBP.
- [ ] Call 10 past guests from the book. Ask for a Google review. Invite a repeat weekday.

**Week 1 exit:** You can quote a stay and an event without asking anyone.

## Week 2 — Assets and the site

- [ ] Book a 1-day pro photographer + drone (or lock a date).
- [ ] Remove dummy pages from srinamofarms.com (EN/DE, layout demos).
- [ ] Add WhatsApp button, map, drive times, veg-only line, 3 packages.
- [ ] One-pager PDF: Stay / Celebrate / Corporate. One page each, or one 2-sided flyer.
- [ ] Import past and future guests into PMS or the Sales sheet.
- [ ] Check-in SOP with caretakers: smile, 15 minutes, manager visible, no payment argument.
- [ ] List 30 planners + 30 photographers + 20 corporates (name + phone). See `02_PARTNER_PROGRAM.md`.
- [ ] First 15 outbound calls. Invite FAM for Saturday of Week 4.

**Week 2 exit:** GBP looks like a real resort. WhatsApp looks like a business.

## Week 3 — Listings

- [ ] Pro shoot happens or phone-content calendar starts (4 Reels this week).
- [ ] Airbnb draft: buyout + 1 room type. Calendar blocked to match the book.
- [ ] MakeMyTrip / Goibibo onboarding started (photos, GST, cancelled-policy).
- [ ] Google review QR printed at reception and restaurant.
- [ ] Feedback form (5 questions) live. Link in WhatsApp.
- [ ] 15 more calls. Log every no.
- [ ] Price-check 4 nearby farm/event venues. Adjust rate card v1.

**Week 3 exit:** At least one listing submitted. Review QR on the counter.

## Week 4 — First FAM and first automation

- [ ] Saturday FAM: 8–12 people, 10:30–14:00, thali + walk + Gaushala.
- [ ] Collect visiting cards. Same-evening WhatsApp with rate card + photo pack.
- [ ] n8n: booking confirmation + checkout thank-you + review link on a test booking.
- [ ] Instagram grid: 9 posts that look like one brand (green, veg, water, cows, sports).
- [ ] Confirm Airbnb / OTA calendar owner (only one person edits).
- [ ] Quote 3 events from FAM or calls.

**Week 4 exit:** Humans who sell weddings have stood on the lawn.

## Week 5–6 — Bookable + weekday story

- [ ] Airbnb live. First OTA live.
- [ ] Publish **Sriperumbudur Offsite** and **Sunday Family Farm** as WhatsApp catalogs.
- [ ] Visit 3 HR/Admin offices or send a physical one-pager via a known employee.
- [ ] 40 calls across the two weeks (planners + HR).
- [ ] Every checkout gets the review WhatsApp. Aim +15 Google reviews in these two weeks.
- [ ] Fix the top service complaint you heard in FAM or reviews (usually check-in or payment).
- [ ] Sunday scoreboard #1 with real numbers.

**Day 42 exit (Phase 1 gate):** Stranger can book. Desk answers in 15 minutes.

## Week 7–8 — Corporate belt

- [ ] Build a 40-name HR/Admin list for Sriperumbudur–Oragadam–Irungattukottai.
- [ ] Offer: 12% weekday org discount, no cash to staff, free cricket or Gaushala slot.
- [ ] 2 site visits for corporates (they come, or you pick them up from the plant gate if needed).
- [ ] Close 1 weekday hold (even a 10-room tester).
- [ ] Photographers: offer 1 free sunrise pre-wedding slot this month in exchange for tags.
- [ ] PMS: every new guest has source + city + occasion.

**Week 8 check:** If zero weekday holds, you are still calling planners only. Split the call list 50/50.

## Week 9–10 — Event machine

- [ ] Wedding package PDF with 2 menus (simple / festive), both veg, pax slabs.
- [ ] Engagement + birthday 4-hour lawn product with a hard end time (protects rooms).
- [ ] 2nd FAM Saturday (new faces only + 2 who ghosted).
- [ ] Checkout automation in production, not test.
- [ ] Lost-enquiry reasons filled for the last 20 misses. Change price or inclusions if “too costly” > 40%.
- [ ] Optional: ₹20–40k Meta test **only if** WhatsApp SLA is green.

## Week 11–12 — Lock the system

- [ ] 80 partners in sheet. Commission policy signed by owners (see partner doc).
- [ ] First commission paid on time (even if small). This is the reputation of the program.
- [ ] Occupancy vs Week 1. If weekend is up and weekday is not, double corporate time in Month 4.
- [ ] Package winners: keep 3, kill the rest.
- [ ] Plan Month 4: paid ads brief, freelance setter job note, Pongal / season calendar.
- [ ] Owners review: continue, add a junior sales caller, or cut a channel.

## Daily operating rhythm (from Week 3)

**Morning (45 min)**  
Unread WhatsApp + missed calls → same-hour reply. Today arrivals. Calendar vs OTA.

**Midday (90 min)**  
Outbound: 8 conversations. Log them.

**Evening (20 min)**  
Tomorrow arrivals message. Content 1 Reel or 5 stories if something real happened (bonfire, cow, sixer).

If you only post and do not call, this sprint has failed even if Instagram looks busy.
