# Partner Program, Call Scripts, and Commission Rules

**Print:** A4 · File → Print → Save as PDF, or open `pdf/02_PARTNER_PROGRAM.pdf`

Print the one-page policy. Do not invent a special deal on the phone.

---

## 1. Policy (owners must sign)

**Srinamo Farms Referral Program — 2026**

- Referral is valid only if the partner’s name / phone is on the enquiry **before** the booking is confirmed.
- Commission is calculated on **collected** venue + room charges, excluding GST, excluding third-party décor the guest paid directly, and excluding OTA bookings.
- F&B: 0% default, or 5% if the partner did not take a catering cut elsewhere. Pick one per partner at onboarding.
- Paid **7 working days after the guest’s final payment**, by UPI / NEFT, with a simple statement.
- No commission on cancelled events after the free-cancel window.
- One partner per booking. If two claim it, the first logged enquiry wins.
- Corporate employees: **discount to the company invoice only**. No personal cash.
- Program can be paused with 15 days’ notice. Bookings already held are honoured.

| Partner type | Rate | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Event / wedding planner | 10% venue + rooms | 8% all-in including F&B |
| Photographer / cine | ₹3,000 flat or 8% | Free sunrise shoot slot instead of cash |
| Decorator / makeup / DJ | 8% | Listed as preferred vendor |
| Travel agent / hotel desk | 12% rooms | 10% if they take OTA-like cancel terms |
| Freelance setter (Phase 5) | 5–8% of closed deal they set | ₹15–25k retainer + 3% |
| Repeat guest | 10% weekday / 5% weekend | Complimentary Gaushala + breakfast |

## 2. Opening script (30 seconds)

Call from a quiet place. Stand, smile, say the name.

> “Hi, I’m [Name] from Srinamo Farms — farm stay and veg celebration venue at Kilachery, near Sriperumbudur and Kanchipuram. About 60–90 minutes from Chennai. Pool, lawn, rooftop, rain dance, cricket, Gaushala. Pure vegetarian kitchen. I’m building a small preferred-partner list. If you send a family, wedding, or corporate, you get 10 percent on venue and rooms after they pay. Can I send a one-pager and invite you this Saturday 10:30 for lunch and a walk-through?”

If they say they already have venues:

> “That’s fine. We are the west-Chennai / Sriperumbudur option when the guest wants veg, sports, and space, not a beach. Keep us for the ones who say Kanchipuram or factory side. I’ll still send the PDF.”

If HR:

> “We run a weekday offsite: rooms or day, veg buffet, cricket or Gaushala, 12 percent off on the company invoice. No personal gift. Useful when your plant visitors or L&D need a place that is not a highway hotel. 40 minutes from most Sriperumbudur gates. Can I mail a one-page rate and host 2 of you for a Tuesday lunch?”

## 3. Follow-up (same day + day 3 + day 10)

1. **Same day:** WhatsApp: 6 photos, PDF, map pin, Saturday FAM time, your name.
2. **Day 3:** “Did you get the PDF? Any date in the next 60 days I should hold unofficially?”
3. **Day 10:** “Last note — if you have a couple who want a pre-wedding sunrise slot, first 3 partners this month get it complimentary.”

Then put them on a 30-day reminder. Stop begging weekly.

## 5. FAM Saturday run-of-show (3.5 hours)

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 10:15 | Water, towel, name stickers |
| 10:30 | 8-minute welcome: veg kitchen, distances, 3 products |
| 10:40 | Walk: rooms (show 2 types only), lawn, rooftop, pool, rain dance, cricket, Gaushala |
| 12:00 | Thali. No long speech. Answer “capacity, rain plan, power, alcohol, rooms on wedding night” |
| 13:15 | Rate card + commission page. Collect 3 preferred dates they already have in mind |
| 13:45 | Photo with the group (they post, you post) |
| 14:00 | Cars out. You WhatsApp the pack by 18:00 |

Never FAM more than 12 people. You want conversation, not a crowd.

## 6. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| Too far | “From Porur / Airport / Sriperumbudur it is a known 60–90 / 40 minute drive. We send the pin and a driver note. Guests who want quiet accept it; beach crowd we do not chase.” |
| No non-veg | “Correct, 100% veg. That is why traditional families, many corporates, and Kanchi guests book us. If they need non-veg we are the wrong venue — we say that in the first minute.” |
| No beach | “Correct. Lake / farm / Gaushala. Different guest.” |
| Cheaper mandapam in Kanchi | “They don’t have this room + pool + sports block. We win when they want stay + function together.” |
| Commission too low | “10% on collected venue+rooms, paid in 7 days, written. We don’t do 20% and then delay.” |
| Can I bring my caterer? | Use your real rule. Suggested: in-house default; outside only with corkage / kitchen fee, still veg-only. |

## 7. What you send after every good call

A single WhatsApp pack, always in this order:

1. 6 photos (aerial or lawn, pool, room, food, Gaushala, rain dance)
2. PDF one-pager
3. Google pin
4. Rate card image
5. Saturday FAM invite **or** “send me a date to hold 48 hours”

If you send a 40-image dump, they will not open it.
